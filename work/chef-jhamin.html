Case study

Chef Jhamin's Kitchen Management System

A full-stack restaurant platform that puts sales, stock, staff and purchasing in one place.

Role
Full-stack development
Stack
React, Express.js, PostgreSQL
Platform
Render, installable PWA

The problem

Small kitchens tend to run on notebooks and memory. Sales sit in one place, stock in another, and staff rotas and supplier orders somewhere else — so nobody can see the state of the business at a glance, and stockouts are discovered too late.

What I built

A full-stack platform covering real-time sales tracking, inventory management with low-stock alerts, staff scheduling, expense tracking, customer and supplier CRM, and purchase orders.

Access and accountability

Money and stock move through several pairs of hands, so the system has role-based access control and a full audit trail. Staff see what their role needs, and every change is attributable.

Decisions that mattered

  • PostgreSQL, because the data is relational and the integrity of stock and sales matters.
  • A PWA build, so the kitchen can install it on a phone or tablet without an app store.
  • Deployed on Render for a straightforward managed deployment.

Outcome

One system of record for the parts of the business that used to be scattered across notebooks.
