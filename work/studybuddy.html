StudyBuddy — BECE Exam Prep

A free, offline-first PWA that explains BECE exam vocabulary in plain language, then drills it with lessons, quizzes and real past papers.

The problem

BECE revision material is dense, and students preparing on shared phones with weak connections cannot rely on always-online resources. They need something that works offline and tells them exactly what to do next.

What I built

A free revision app covering seven subjects: Mathematics, Integrated Science, English Language, Social Studies, French, ICT and Ghanaian Language (Akan).

  • 745 glossary terms, each with a plain-language definition, example and difficulty level.
  • A stairs path per subject. Every lesson ends in a mini-quiz, with a summit mega-quiz at the top.
  • Four question types — multiple choice, true/false, fill-the-blank and matching — at easy, medium and hard levels.
  • Real BECE past papers from 2023 to 2025, with year selection and timed practice.

Content and design

The curriculum follows the official NaCCA Common Core Programme for B7–B9, and the content lives in JSON so it can grow without touching code.

  • One clear next step. The stairs make the next lesson obvious rather than presenting a content dump.
  • Progress you can feel. XP and levels, daily streaks, hearts, earnable themes in a store, and a progress report you can print or save.
  • Offline first. A service worker and device-local progress keep the app usable with no connection after the first load.

How it is built

React 18 with Vite and hash-based routing, which stays robust on a static host with no server. Progress is saved on the device, with Firebase handling accounts and backup. It deploys to Vercel on every push to master.

Outcome

A revision app that opens on a weak connection and tells a student what to do next.
