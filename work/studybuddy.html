The problem

BECE revision material is dense, and students preparing on shared phones with weak connections cannot rely on always-online resources. They need something that works offline and tells them exactly what to do next.

What I built

A free revision app covering seven subjects: Mathematics, Integrated Science, English Language, Social Studies, French, ICT and Ghanaian Language (Akan).

01

745 glossary terms

Each with a plain-language definition, example and difficulty level.

02

A stairs path per subject

Every lesson ends in a mini-quiz, with a summit mega-quiz at the top.

03

Four question types

Multiple choice, true/false, fill-the-blank and matching, at easy, medium and hard levels.

04

Real BECE past papers

2023 to 2025, with year selection and timed practice.

Content and design

The curriculum follows the official NaCCA Common Core Programme for B7 to B9, and the content lives in JSON so it can grow without touching code.

One clear next step

The stairs make the next lesson obvious rather than presenting a content dump.

Progress you can feel

XP and levels, daily streaks, hearts, earnable themes in a store, and a progress report you can print or save.

Offline first

A service worker and device-local progress keep the app usable with no connection after the first load.
