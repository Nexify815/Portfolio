StudyBuddy — BECE Exam Prep

A gamified exam-prep PWA that gives Ghanaian Junior High students one clear next step.

Role
Design & development
Stack
React, Vite, Firebase
Platform
Offline-first PWA
Open the live app View the showcase

The problem

BECE revision material is often dense, and the students who need it most are the least equipped to navigate it. With limited connectivity and shared devices, a revision tool has to work offline and tell the student exactly what to do next.

What I built

An offline-first PWA covering 7 subjects, 500+ glossary terms explained in plain language, step-by-step learning paths, gamified quizzes, and real past-paper questions from 2023 to 2025.

Design decisions

  • One clear next step. Every screen answers what to do right now, instead of presenting a content dump.
  • Progress you can feel. XP, streaks, hearts and weekly leagues borrow the grammar of games students already play, aimed at consistency rather than cramming.
  • Built for a phone on a weak connection. A mobile-first layout and a service worker keep the app usable offline.

Outcome

A revision app a student can open on a weak connection and use without having to decide where to start.
