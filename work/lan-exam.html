Case study

Offline-First LAN Examination System

A self-hosted exam platform that runs a whole school's assessment day with no internet connection at all.

Role
Design & full-stack development
Deployment
Self-hosted, LAN-only
Platform
Web app for student devices

The problem

Assessment days are the worst possible time to depend on the internet. A school with unreliable connectivity cannot risk a cloud-hosted exam platform failing mid-paper, and paper exams bring their own problems: marking load, transcription errors, and results that take days.

What I built

A digital assessment platform that is self-hosted and designed to deploy and manage examinations completely without external internet. It includes an intelligent bulk-import engine for standardised Excel/CSV student lists and complex question banks, and it serves sandboxed, secure assessments to any device on the local network, grading locally and in real time.

How it works

  • Staff import student lists and question banks in bulk from Excel/CSV, matched to the formats schools already use.
  • The server runs on the school's own network and issues assessment sessions to whatever devices are available.
  • Grading happens locally and immediately, so results do not wait on a round trip.
  • Sessions are sandboxed so candidates stay inside the paper.

Decisions that mattered

  • Offline-first as the core constraint, not a fallback. Every feature works without an external connection.
  • Bulk import over manual entry. A school's real data already lives in spreadsheets; the system should meet it there.
  • Local grading. The point of the system is results on the day, not later.

Outcome

An assessment day that depends only on the local network — no internet, no third-party service, and no waiting for results.
