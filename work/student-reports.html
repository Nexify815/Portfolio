Case study

Student Report Automation & PDF Export

Turns marks in a Google Sheet into formatted PDF report cards in Drive — in batches, on a schedule.

Role
Design & development
Stack
Apps Script, Sheets, Drive
Platform
Cloud automation

The problem

Report cards are repetitive, deadline-driven work. Producing them by hand for a whole year group invites inconsistent formatting and copy-paste errors — and the school already keeps its marks in spreadsheets.

What I built

A generator that reads assessment data from Google Sheets and produces formatted STEM report cards, exporting them as PDFs to Google Drive. It is configurable per school: swap the logo and folder IDs in config cells and it works for a different school.

The hard part

Apps Script caps a single execution at roughly six minutes. A year group's worth of reports can exceed that, so generation runs in batches driven by time-based triggers, picking up where the last run stopped.

Decisions that mattered

  • Config cells instead of code edits, so a non-developer can point it at a new school.
  • Batched runs on triggers, to stay inside the platform's execution limit.
  • PDF output into Drive, because that is how the reports are actually distributed.

Outcome

Report generation runs unattended on a schedule instead of occupying a staff member for a day.
